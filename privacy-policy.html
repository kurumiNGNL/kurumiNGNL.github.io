Privacy Policy — Neuro Matrix

Developer: verzocktGames • Package: com.neuro.matrix • Effective Date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how verzocktGames ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in the mobile game Neuro Matrix (package ID: com.neuro.matrix).

1. Local-Only Game Storage (No User Accounts)

Neuro Matrix does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles. Your high scores, virtual currencies (Credits, Stars, Crowns), unlocked skins, tile covers, wallpapers, audio settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android local storage and Capacitor Preferences. We do not operate external game servers to collect or store your personal gameplay data.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Collected

Neuro Matrix integrates trusted Google services that may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display rewarded and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect and share:
    • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
    • App interactions (ad views, clicks, and video completion events)
    • Crash logs and Diagnostics (app performance and ad-loading telemetry)
    • Device or other IDs (such as your Android Advertising ID / AD_ID and App Set ID)
    This data is used for advertising and marketing, analytics, frequency capping, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Optional one-time in-app purchases (cosmetic unlocks and virtual currency packs) are processed securely by Google Play. We receive purchase verification tokens and purchase history status from Google Play solely to grant and restore your unlocked items. We never receive or store your credit card or payment details.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All network communications performed by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google Play Billing are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

4. Your Privacy & Consent Choices

  • Ad Consent (EEA / UK / Applicable US States): Where required by law, consent preferences are collected via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and can be updated at any time inside the app's Settings → Privacy Policy → Manage Ad Privacy.
  • Advertising ID Controls: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).
  • Local Data Removal: You can reset local high scores in the app's Settings menu, or delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Neuro Matrix.

5. Children's Privacy

Neuro Matrix is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if app features or legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
